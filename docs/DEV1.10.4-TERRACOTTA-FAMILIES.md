# WEM 5.0.0-dev1.10.4 – standalone terracotta families

- Registry IDs now match the standalone texture families: `*_bricked_terracotta` and `*_terracotta_bricks`.
- Legacy `*_terracotta_brick` and `*_bricks` registry IDs for these two decorative families were replaced rather than duplicated.
- `lightblue`/`silver` legacy naming is normalized to `light_blue`/`light_gray` inside these two families.
- Uncolored Bricked Terracotta and Terracotta Bricks were added, including slab, stairs and wall variants.
- Latest user-provided Bricked Terracotta PNGs and the previously provided Terracotta Bricks PNGs are used directly as complete standalone textures.
- No overlay/composite texture model is used.
