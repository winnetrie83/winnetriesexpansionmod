WEM 5.0.0-dev1.16.9

- Fixed Apple hanging-fruit rendering by copying the vanilla apple sprite into WEM's block texture atlas.
- Fruits and Drinks remain separate config modules.
- Optional-fruit drinks require both Drinks and Fruits for normal availability. Apple drinks remain available with Drinks alone.
- When Fruits is enabled, Apple Trees are the apple source: vanilla apple drops are removed from Oak and Dark Oak leaves.
- When Fruits is disabled, WEM fruit-tree content is unavailable and vanilla Oak/Dark Oak apple mechanics are untouched.
- Current drink items still have no brewing/crafting recipes yet; future fruit-drink recipes must carry both module conditions.
