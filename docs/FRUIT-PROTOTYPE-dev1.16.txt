WEM dev1.16 fruit prototype

Implemented a first experimental fruits module:
- New module toggle: fruits
- Fruit items: orange, peach, pear, plum, lemon, cherry
- Saplings: orange, peach, pear, plum, lemon
- New blocks: matching fruit leaves and hanging fruit blocks
- Saplings now grow into simple handcrafted oak-based fruit trees
- Fruit leaves occasionally spawn hanging fruit below them
- Hanging fruits are harvested by breaking them
- Vanilla cherry leaves now have a small chance to drop cherries when broken without shears

Notes:
- This is intentionally a prototype; tree shape, balancing and natural worldgen can still be refined later.
- Fruit leaves currently drop their sapling directly to make testing easier.
