WEM 5.0.0-dev1.16.4 fruit prototype fixes

- Fruit leaves and hanging-fruit blocks no longer register BlockItems.
- Fruit sapling BlockItems remain; these are the actual plantable sapling items.
- Saplings explicitly accept grass block, dirt, coarse dirt, podzol, rooted dirt, mycelium, farmland and moss (plus the dirt tag).
- Fixed tree growth: the old space check incorrectly treated the sapling itself as an obstruction.
- Bonemeal is temporarily deterministic during prototyping: one use grows the tree if enough space exists.
