WEM 5.0.0-dev1.10.2 standalone texture migration

- Layer-composed block/item models have been removed from active resources.
- User-supplied Bricked Terracotta PNGs are direct textures for the *_terracotta_brick family.
- User-supplied Terracotta Bricks PNGs are direct textures for the legacy *_bricks family.
- Registry IDs are retained for saved-world compatibility. Display names now reflect Bricked Terracotta / Terracotta Bricks.
- Carved/cracked terracotta and concrete variants for which no full PNG exists currently render with their plain material base only. Their IDs/recipes/loot remain intact until replacement standalone textures are supplied.
- Future block visuals should be supplied as complete standalone PNGs.
