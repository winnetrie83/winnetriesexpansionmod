WEM 5.0.0-dev1.16.7 fruit regrowth fix

Root cause:
Healthy LeavesBlock states do not provide a reliable randomTick stream for crop-style fruit regrowth.

New behaviour:
- Right-click harvesting schedules the exact supporting leaf for regrowth.
- Regrowth delay is randomized between 30 and 60 seconds.
- A lightweight recovery scan around players can restore fruit that was manually broken or lost across a restart.
- Recovery applies to apple/orange/peach/pear/plum/lemon fruit leaves and vanilla cherry leaves.
- Recovery density is capped locally so every underside leaf does not eventually become fruit-covered.
