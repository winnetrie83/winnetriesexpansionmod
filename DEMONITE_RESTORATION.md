# Demonite restoration — 5.0.0-dev1.9

- Source of truth: user-provided `ExpansionMod-2.3.30(MC1.11.2).jar`.
- Restored exact original textures: `demonite.png`, `demonite_smooth.png`, `demonite_bricked.png`.
- Removed reconstructed Demonite carved/cracked variants.
- Removed the obsolete reconstructed metal blocks/items, their equipment, recipes, tags, textures, models and worldgen.
- Demonite now generates only in Nether biomes and replaces netherrack.
- Generation intentionally follows the old 1.11.2 design closely: broad Y range and abundant large veins.
- Smooth Demonite is obtainable by smelting raw Demonite; Demonite Bricks keep the existing 2x2 recipe.
